Cookies, fonts, and hosting

The connected form uses a necessary HttpOnly session cookie to verify submissions and show a receipt. It has no advertising analytics or tracking cookies. Fonts and photographs are served locally. Form values are not stored in browser local storage; submitted requests are saved in the receiving workspace’s application database.

This first release runs on this computer. Its links are not publicly reachable until hosting is configured. The website guide remains a preset guide and does not send chat messages to the inbox; complete the walkthrough form to submit a request.
