Walkthrough requests

This connected website sends the form to {{ business.name }}’s private workspace. Submitting saves your name, business name, email address, trade, package interest and message in the application’s database on this computer. Authorized members of that workspace can view the request, add internal notes and manage follow-up. Other businesses’ workspaces cannot view it.

A confirmation appears only after the request is saved. It is not an appointment confirmation or an automatic email. Avoid submitting passwords, payment details or sensitive customer records. Contact the receiving business to discuss corrections or deletion of your saved request. This release has no automated retention or deletion schedule.
