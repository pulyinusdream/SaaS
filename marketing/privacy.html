How this website handles your request and your conversation with the website guide.

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Your walkthrough request

The contact form prepares an email draft addressed to vicentepaulandrei1823@gmail.com. This website does not submit the form to a server, store the details in a database, or send an email automatically. You choose whether to open the draft and send it from your own email app.

The request includes your name, business name, email address, trade, package preference, and any message you enter. Avoid including passwords, payment information, or sensitive customer records. Once you send an email, its handling also depends on your email provider and the recipient’s email service.

The website chat

The KJAncy guide provides preset answers from the published service information. It is not a live agent or generative AI service. Messages stay in the current page’s memory and are not sent to a server or AI provider. Closing the panel preserves the conversation on this page; Start over, reloading, or leaving the page clears it. Avoid entering sensitive personal information. The guide cannot access a live calendar, confirm an appointment, or send a message. Contact links open the walkthrough form or your email app for you to complete the request.

Copying and downloading

If you choose Copy request, the prepared text is copied to your clipboard when your browser permits it. If you choose Download request, a text file is saved to your device. The website does not upload that file. Form details stay in the current page’s memory and are lost when you reload or leave the page.

Cookies, fonts, and hosting

The website’s own code does not add tracking cookies, advertising analytics, or browser storage. Fonts and photographs are served as local website assets. No third-party font request is needed to read the page. Your hosting provider and browser may process technical connection information necessary to deliver the website. Any sign-in required to view a private hosted version is handled by the hosting platform.

Illustrative examples

The interactive walkthrough illustrates a proposed customer handoff. They are not live customer records, confirmed appointments, testimonials, or guarantees of booking results.

Packages and coverage

Prices are in U.S. dollars. Both listed packages have a $500 one-time setup fee. Human follow-up and live phone coverage are provided during agreed coverage hours. The Lead Booking System does not include live incoming phone answering. The Dedicated Dispatcher System is not a 24/7 service. Final scope, working hours, tools, and expected lead or call volume are confirmed before onboarding. Ad spend and ongoing SEO campaigns are not included.

Questions about this website

Contact vicentepaulandrei1823@gmail.com with questions about this site or information you have emailed. This notice describes the current website; a future CRM connection or other collection method will require an updated notice.
