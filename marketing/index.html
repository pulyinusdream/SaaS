A few good questions.

The questions worth asking, with the answers in plain view.

Who answers our calls?
The Dedicated Dispatcher System includes a person answering incoming calls during agreed working hours. The Lead Booking System follows up with new online inquiries; it does not answer incoming calls.
Already have a website?
You may not need a new one. We can discuss improving your existing website or building a simple site that makes appointment requests easier.
Can AI book by itself?
Chat can answer basic questions and collect or help schedule appointment requests. A person handles follow-up or confirmation when needed.
Is phone support 24/7?
No. We agree on coverage hours and expected call volume before onboarding. Your website and lead form can still collect inquiries after hours.
Are ads included?
Paid ads and ongoing SEO campaigns are not included. We focus on responding to inquiries and tracking appointments, without guaranteeing job totals.
How do we get started?
We discuss your trade, current tools, coverage needs, and expected lead volume. Then we confirm the package, setup scope, and handoff process together.
