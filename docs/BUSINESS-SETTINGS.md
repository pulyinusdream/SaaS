# Business settings and guided setup

Approved scope: the user accepted owner-managed business profile, services/service areas, shared timezone, and guided setup before deployment. Extend the existing Flask/SQLite/Jinja app inline; preserve mint framed sheet seed716491800, IBM Plex Sans, SVG icons, customer records and security. No dependency, upload, external mail, payments or hosting.

Owners use /settings to edit business name (1–100), public email (up to254) and phone (up to40, at least one valid contact), services (1–20 unique lines, each1–60), service areas (1–20 unique lines, each1–100), and existing allowed timezone. Areas are informational; customer addresses remain free-form. Contact details are explicitly labelled public. Defaults retain existing six services and blank contact/areas without claiming setup is complete. Historical leads retain original service/location strings. Marketing walkthrough trade categories remain unchanged because they describe the requesting business, not the receiving company's service catalogue.

Add settings columns in the existing transactional migration, leaving the base schema compatible with old fixtures. All owner writes lock/recheck membership and role; settings saves check settings_version. Existing timezone writes increment/check that version when supplied. Ignore submitted tenant IDs. New service requests/manual leads validate against current company services under the insertion lock. A removed choice returns a clear error; old saved leads remain readable. Keep shared timezone instants unchanged.

/setup answers 'What remains before this business can receive inquiries?' Three required steps derive from profile/catalogue completeness, an explicit owner acknowledgement of sharing the public form, and actual saved public/website inquiries. Optional team row reports current members/pending invitations truthfully and does not block solo owners. Copy/open link controls use the real form URL; no clipboard or page visit is represented as external delivery. Owner can clear their sharing acknowledgement. A compact inbox notice links to setup until complete; no forced redirect or fake customer/test data.

Settings: one labelled form with profile, catalogue and timezone fieldsets; restrained anchor navigation/support context. Setup: ordered actionable rows, actual three-step progress, optional team action and share-link panel. Existing validation summary, inline errors, pending/success/disabled states and mobile navigation are reused. Responsive single column on phones, 44px actions, focus and reduced-motion support. No decorative metrics/charts.

Review focus: stale settings vs timezone forms; role revoked after identity loading; public validation while catalogue changes; tenant-bound settings/checklist/acknowledgement; no migration mutation of saved records or fake setup completion.

Final review decisions: public phones require7–15 main digits and optional1–8 extension digits; mailto addresses are percent-encoded. New lead origins use explicit manual/customer_form metadata set by the server. Legacy service records use existing inquiry activity without rewriting history; old actor display-name collisions cannot be fully resolved. Phone layouts put copy/open actions in the sharing step and expose a native shortcut to the service request form.
