# Reports and CSV export

Approved scope: the user accepted lead summaries, scheduled/cancelled appointment reports, date/service/teammate filters and owner-only CSV export. Extend Flask/SQLite/Jinja inline without a dependency, database migration, artificial metrics or external service. Preserve all customer data and the mint framed-sheet design, IBM Plex Sans and SVG icons.

Report semantics: owner-only /reports with kind=leads or appointments. Lead rows filter received timestamps and show current lead status; Closed does not mean a won sale. Appointment rows filter slots overlapping the selected dates and show current scheduled/cancelled state, irrespective of lead receipt date. Reschedules use the latest saved slot, not the history of every change. Dates are inclusive in the shared workspace timezone; query bounds use local midnight and next midnight converted to UTC. Default is the last30 local dates including today. Accept2000–2100 and at most366 dates. No revenue, funnel conversion, historical status reconstruction or cancellation-event totals are inferred.

Services include current catalogue and historical lead service strings in this company. Teammates include current members and people referenced by this company's saved assignments, including former teammates; All and Unassigned are explicit. Lead teammate means lead responsibility; appointment teammate means attending person. Query all aggregates/rows from one scoped read transaction after refreshing/rechecking owner membership/timezone. Sort/paginate25 rows and show true counts. Invalid filters show retained fields/errors without false zero metrics.

CSV exports cover all filtered rows, not just a page. Bound at10000 rows; refuse oversized exports clearly without truncation. UTF-8 BOM, quoted cells, CRLF rows and offset-bearing local ISO timestamps with a workspace timezone column. Export only report/customer/assignment fields, excluding internal notes, messages, passwords, tokens and private account details. Prefix spreadsheet formula-like text (including whitespace/control and normalized fullwidth prefixes) with an apostrophe; customer strings may be preserved with this protective text prefix, phone numbers included. This is data export, not a guarantee about every spreadsheet application's import behavior.

Export route /reports/export.csv consumes a30-minute signed link binding workspace, timezone and exact normalized report filters. Recheck current owner under the read snapshot. A workspace/timezone switch or invalid/expired token rejects the link and asks for a fresh report. Export uses latest committed rows for those filters, not a frozen copy of the earlier screen. Submitted company/filter overrides do not change signed scope. Responses remain no-store and attachment filenames contain only validated dates/kind. Empty exports contain headers only.

Interface: Reports sidebar link for owners, Leads/Appointments tabs, labelled date/service/teammate fields, restrained current-state summary strip and semantic paged table. Explain receipt-date versus slot-date semantics beside the results. Export action shows full filtered count; disabled with explanation when oversized/invalid. Empty state offers filter reset/inbox. Existing pending/focused errors, keyboard/reduced-motion/mobile navigation apply. Phone rows stack with labels; no page overflow or decorative chart/KPI tiles.

Review focus: tenant/role revocation and signed export scope; UTC/local/DST and cross-midnight windows; list/summary/export agreement across pagination; CSV formula/delimiter/Unicode handling and private-field exclusions; removed services/teammates and misleading historical metrics.
